Community Events
================

We occasionally promote community-hosted events! If you have experience with hosting events or other activities for fellow players, you are welcome to reach out about receiving some additional support from us.

Tips for Hosting Events
-----------------------

You may find the following advice helpful when hosting an event:

#. **Make your contest accessible.** Avoid complicated rules!

#. **Keep it a reasonable duration.** Longer contests can struggle with retention and scheduling.

#. **Link to helpful pages.** E.g., modding tutorials for modding events!

#. **Have a way to communicate.** Such as a webpage, sign-up form, or Discord server.

#. **Don't participate in your own event!** Let players shine.

Many players are interested in community-ran events. Focus on providing the information they need to get started!
